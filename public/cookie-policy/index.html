Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how AutoGlaze Stoke uses cookies and similar technologies on this website.

Cookies are small files stored on your device. Some are needed to make the website work properly. Others are optional and are only used if you choose to allow them.

Essential Cookies

Essential cookies and similar storage help the website function, remember your cookie choice, support enquiry forms, and protect forms from spam or automated misuse. These are necessary for the website and cannot be switched off through our cookie controls.

Optional Cookies

Optional cookies are used for embedded services such as Google Maps. We block Google Maps until you accept optional cookies. If you reject optional cookies, you can still use the website and open our address directly in Google Maps from the map placeholder.

Cloudflare Turnstile

We use Cloudflare Turnstile on enquiry forms as a necessary security check. It helps confirm that form submissions are genuine and protects the website from spam. This is treated as an essential security feature for the form.

Google Maps

When you accept optional cookies and view an embedded Google Map, Google may process information such as your IP address, browser information and map usage. Google’s own privacy and cookie terms apply to that service.

Changing Your Choice

You can change your cookie choice at any time by using the Cookie Settings button in the footer or the floating Cookie Settings button after you have made a choice.
